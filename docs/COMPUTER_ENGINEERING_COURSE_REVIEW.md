# Computer Engineering course review

This sanitized draft contains 36 course offerings transcribed from four University of Lagos student course registration forms. The project owner confirms these forms collectively contain **all Computer Engineering courses for 100 and 200 Level**. The records are assigned to the Computer Engineering department in the platform.

## Imported scope

- 2024/2025 First and Second semesters are mapped to 100 Level.
- 2025/2026 First and Second semesters are mapped to 200 Level.
- Each form names the programme as **Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering**, while its `DEPARTMENT` field says **Electrical and Electronics Engineering**. The CSV preserves the printed source department; the course records are placed under Computer Engineering as confirmed by the project owner.
- Course codes, titles and credit units are transcribed as printed.

The CSV intentionally excludes the student's name, registration number, portrait and signature fields. Seed records contain no student information.
